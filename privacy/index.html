Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

Overview

strikethroughtextgenerators.com ("we," "us," "this site") provides a free strikethrough text generator and related guides. This policy explains what information is collected when you use the site and how it's handled.

Text you enter into the generator

Text you type or paste into the generator is processed entirely in your browser using JavaScript. It is not sent to, stored on, or logged by our servers. Uploading a .txt file works the same way — the file is read locally by your browser and never transmitted.

Local storage

We use your browser's local storage to remember one preference: whether you've selected dark mode. This stays on your device and is not accessible to us.

Analytics and cookies

We may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand aggregate traffic (such as page views and general location at a country level) in order to improve the site. This data is not tied to your name or used to identify you individually. If we add cookie-based analytics or advertising in the future, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Third-party links

Our blog posts may link to third-party apps and services (for example, referencing how strikethrough works in various software). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties — review their own policies separately.

Contact form

Our contact page opens a pre-filled email in your own email client. We do not store form submissions on a server; the message goes directly from you to info@strikethroughtextgenerators.com.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent to info@strikethroughtextgenerators.com.
